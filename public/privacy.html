Privacy Policy

Hermes Personal Assistant · Effective date: November 5, 2026

Hermes is a personal AI assistant that automates routine tasks for a single user — the account owner — on their own Google account. This policy explains what Google data the application accesses and how it is handled.

What the app accesses

When you authorize Hermes, you grant access to the following Google services, scoped to your own account:

  • Gmail — read, send, and organize email for personal automation and reminders
  • Google Calendar — read and create events
  • Google Drive, Docs, and Sheets — read and create files
  • Google Contacts — read-only

How your data is used

Your Google data is used solely to perform tasks you ask for — for example, drafting or sending an email you request, reading your calendar, checking for event invitations, or creating files you asked it to create. The application acts as your agent; it does not use your data for any purpose you did not request.

Data sharing

Your data is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party, and is never used for advertising.

Data storage and access

Authorization credentials are stored locally on your own machine. Email, calendar, contact, and file data is read directly from your own Google account at the time a task runs; no separate copy of your mailbox or files is retained.

Revoking access

You can revoke this application’s access at any time from your Google Account’s third-party app permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
